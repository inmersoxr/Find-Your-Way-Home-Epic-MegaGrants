10 / PEOPLE & TIME

Places hold
people.
Places hold
memory.

3D Gaussian Splatting reconstructs captured places from images, preserving their visual richness and irregularity. These real spaces become spatial material for the game.

4D capture extends that idea into movement: an artisan at work, a musician playing, someone crossing a street. Historic architecture can also connect to another moment of its own past. Presence and memory become part of the puzzle.
