Concept imagery

The four frames used in the illustrated journey were generated for this project as speculative concept art: a familiar Cuenca courtyard, an Amazon destination, incompatible gravity, and altered scale. They are not exact documentary locations, completed Gaussian captures or actual gameplay. The complete prompt and provenance are recorded in CONCEPT_ASSETS.md in the repository. Earlier portal illustrations are archived and no longer used on the main page.

Conceptual references

M. C. Escher, Relativity (1953), National Gallery of Art. The other games named on the page are linked to their respective official sites. No reference artwork is reproduced here.
