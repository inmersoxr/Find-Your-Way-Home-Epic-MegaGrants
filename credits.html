Concept imagery

The first door, Cotopaxi threshold, Amazon threshold, Galápagos threshold, Look Up, and Impossible Ecuador were generated for this project as speculative concept visualizations. Real source photographs are not represented as the direct photographic inputs to those generated scenes.
