# Narrative contract

This file records the project owner's original brief and subsequent corrections. Use it before changing copy, imagery or interaction.

## Game, not technology showcase

Find Your Way Home is a surreal exploration puzzle game. Its objective is to find the way home. Gaussian capture supplies believable places and people; the game breaks the relationships between those realities.

## The essential progression

1. Begin in an ordinary real place.
2. Cross an ordinary architectural threshold and emerge in a different real place.
3. Attempt to return through the same threshold. The previous place is no longer there. The crossing is not an automatically reversible A–B connection.
4. Encounter incompatible spatial relationships: another world overhead, local gravity, rotated orientation, radically altered scale, time and world state.
5. Observe, recognize patterns and learn the rules of the geography. It is not arbitrary randomness.
6. The goal remains finding the way home.

Do not show the previous room preserved as a visible portal after crossing. Do not invent a final puzzle solution, an orientation switch that guarantees home, or specific keys/items as confirmed mechanics. Conditions such as keys, hats and time appear in references and examples, not an approved mechanics specification.

## Ecuador and expansion

Ecuador is the first universe and source of spatial postcards, because of its compact concentration of environmental, architectural and cultural variety. It is not the permanent geographical limit. The same system can expand with captured places and people worldwide.
